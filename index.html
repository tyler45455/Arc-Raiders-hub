Update History

Frozen Trail 2.0 - October 8, 2026

Described as the biggest update since launch. Highlights from the official patch notes and community write-ups.

New Map and Conditions

  • Pendola Pass - new frozen mountain map beyond the Rust Belt
  • Frigate - major new ARC encounter and map condition
  • Redirection - new map condition

New ARC

  • Bully
  • Skulker
  • Hydra

New Weapons

  • Bantam
  • Stiletto
  • Amplified Weapons - upgraded versions of existing weapons

New Gear

  • Grappling Hook
  • Basic Camera and Advanced Camera
  • Tether Launcher
  • Yank Grenade
  • Mountaineer's Detector
  • Banjo and Harmonica

Per the wiki loot table, Basic Camera, Advanced Camera and Banjo cannot be recycled.

The Outpost

  • New Outpost settlement with a Research Bench
  • Lootable "Design" items unlock craftable Outpost furniture (about 100 designs)
  • Celeste now trades materials for furniture and the new gameplay items

Progression

  • Skill Tree fully revamped (choose one branch, pick from three skills at branch ends)
  • Reward Pass replaces Raider Decks; Cred removed and replaced by Reward Points
  • Frozen Trail Pass: 60 levels, Free and Premium tracks
  • 22 new quests
  • Max stash size +2 levels (328 max without Expedition bonuses)

Weapon Stencils

  • 14 stencils total: 5 Premium Pass, 1 DLC, 8 found Topside
  • Learn them like blueprints, apply to compatible weapons
  • Applying needs Stencil Parts (found Topside)
  • Stenciled weapons can still be looted if you are downed

Other Changes

  • New Scrambling move to sprint up steep slopes
  • Reworked Map Selection screen and new default gamepad mapping
  • Riven Tides loot value up about 15%
  • Drop loot to the ground directly from containers
  • Collector Set DLC available

Balance Changes (selected)

ItemChange
HullcrackerExplosive damage reworked: over 50% more to armor plates, about 30% less to fragile parts

Sources: official Embark Studios patch notes (arcraiders.com) plus community summaries. Details can change with hotfixes - check the official notes for the latest.
