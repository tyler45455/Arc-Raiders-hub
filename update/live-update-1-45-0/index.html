Live Update 1.45.0

Released Sep 8, 2026Version 1.45.0

Expedition 5 begins and the end of Cred is announced.

Key changes

  • Raider Project Ascending the Mountain, the Hornet Hunter Set and the start of Expedition 5.
  • Cred stays usable until Oct 8, then expires with Frozen Trail; claimed rewards are kept.
  • Starting an Expedition no longer resets quests by default; you choose whether to reset.
  • New severe over-encumbrance tier limits you to walking; Aphelion weapon sway reduced.
  • Unreal Engine upgraded from 5.3 to 5.7; several PS5 and general crash fixes.
  • Known issue: PC crashes tied to Windows Update KB5121003, with a workaround provided.
