Arc Raiders Hub

Look up what any item recycles or salvages into, track your materials, and catch up on every update through Frozen Trail 2.0.

On iPhone or iPad: tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”. On other browsers, open the browser menu and choose Install or Add to Home Screen.

Recycling lookup

Search an item to see what it breaks down into, or search a material to see every item that gives it, biggest yield first.

Add or correct an item

For items the list is missing or numbers that changed in a patch. Saved in this browser only. Format: 6 Plastic Parts, 1 Processor

Material tracker

Add materials by hand, or press "Add to tracker" on any item in the lookup. Set a goal for a material to see progress, and the lookup will show which items help you reach it. Saved in this browser only.

Shopping list

Built automatically from your active quests, saved projects, workshop upgrades and tracker goals, compared with what you track. Type how many you have in each box to update your tracker.

Workshop planner

Tick how many levels of each station you have finished. Each station’s next level goes on the shopping list unless you untick “Include”.

History

One point per day, saved in this browser as you use the tracker.

Back up or move your data

Reset everything to default (the red button above) clears quests, tracker, goals, projects, checklists, notes, pins, loadouts and every other saved setting back to zero. It keeps your language, theme and contrast choices. Export a backup first if you might want it back.

Your tracker, goals, starred items, projects and checklist are saved only in this browser. Press Export to get a block of text you can keep, then paste it here on another device and press Import.

Automatic snapshots

This browser keeps its three most recent snapshots, taken when you open the site at least 12 hours after the last one.

Frozen Trail 2.0

Released October 8, 2026. The biggest update since launch, based on the official Embark patch notes and community write-ups.

New map and conditions

  • Pendola Pass, a frozen mountain map beyond the Rust Belt
  • Frigate, a major ARC encounter and map condition
  • Redirection, a new map condition

New ARC

  • Bully
  • Skulker
  • Hydra

New weapons

  • Bantam
  • Stiletto
  • Amplified Weapons, upgraded versions of existing weapons

New gear

  • Grappling Hook
  • Basic and Advanced Camera
  • Tether Launcher
  • Yank Grenade
  • Mountaineer's Detector
  • Banjo and Harmonica

The Outpost

  • New settlement with a Research Bench
  • Lootable Design items unlock craftable furniture (about 100 designs)
  • Celeste now trades materials for furniture and the new gear

Progression

  • Skill Tree fully revamped
  • Reward Pass replaces Raider Decks; Cred is removed and replaced by Reward Points
  • Frozen Trail Pass: 60 levels, Free and Premium tracks
  • 22 new quests; stash size +2 levels (328 max without Expedition bonuses)

Weapon Stencils

  • 14 stencils: 5 Premium Pass, 1 DLC, 8 found Topside
  • Learn like blueprints, apply to compatible weapons
  • Applying needs Stencil Parts, found Topside
  • A stenciled weapon can still be looted if you go down

Other changes

  • New Scrambling move for steep slopes
  • Reworked Map Selection screen and gamepad defaults
  • Riven Tides loot value up about 15%
  • You can drop loot to the ground straight from containers

Weapons & gear

Stats for every weapon plus augments, shields, healing, utility, grenades, traps and mines, from the ARC Raiders Wiki. A dash means the wiki does not list that value. Sell prices are in Coins; weapons list upgrade levels I to IV. Rarity, weight and sell price for gear come from each item’s own wiki page. Grenade damage is as listed on the wiki; a dash means the grenade does no listed damage.

Quests

All 107 trader quests with objectives, hand-in items and rewards. A quest stays hidden until you complete the one before it, so tick quests off to unlock the next.

Reference

Enemies, trader stock, workshop upgrades, the skill tree and blueprints. A dash means the wiki lists no value.

Tools

The shopping list and workshop planner are now in the Tracker tab. A pre-raid checklist, a project helper that compares what you need with your tracker, and a side-by-side item compare. Saved in this browser only.

Raid checklist

Tick things off before you drop in. Edit the list to match how you play.

Daily and weekly tasks

Tick things off and they reset on their own: daily tasks at midnight, weekly tasks on Monday (your device’s time).

Project helper

Pick a project below to add its stages from the wiki, or type your own. Each saved stage shows how close your tracker is. Custom format: 200 Metal Parts, 50 Wires

Compare weapons

Pick two weapons to see their stats side by side. The better value in each row is highlighted.

Loadout builder

Choose an augment, shield, weapons and items to see your carried weight against the augment’s weight limit.

Where do I farm this?

Pick a material to see how many you still need, what needs it, and which items give the most.

Stash space planner

An estimate only: the wiki does not list stack sizes, so this counts slots you type in. Your stash size is in the game’s stash screen.

Keep or recycle?

Sorts every item using your active quests, saved projects and tracker goals. Not a replacement for judgement, but it catches the items you would regret recycling.

Compare items

Pick two items to see what each breaks down into, using the Recycle or Salvage setting from the lookup tab.

Maps

Pick a map to see its difficulty, unlock requirement, conditions and what changed there. Detailed layouts are on the wiki; each card links to that map's page.
