Arc Raiders Hub

Look up what any item recycles or salvages into, track your materials, and catch up on every update through Frozen Trail 2.0.

Recycling lookup

Search an item to see what it breaks down into, or search a material to see every item that gives it, biggest yield first.

Add or correct an item

For items the list is missing or numbers that changed in a patch. Saved in this browser only. Format: 6 Plastic Parts, 1 Processor

Material tracker

Add materials by hand, or press "Add to tracker" on any item in the lookup. Set a goal for a material to see progress, and the lookup will show which items help you reach it. Saved in this browser only.

Back up or move your data

Your tracker, goals, starred items, projects and checklist are saved only in this browser. Press Export to get a block of text you can keep, then paste it here on another device and press Import.

Frozen Trail 2.0

Released October 8, 2026. The biggest update since launch, based on the official Embark patch notes and community write-ups.

New map and conditions

  • Pendola Pass, a frozen mountain map beyond the Rust Belt
  • Frigate, a major ARC encounter and map condition
  • Redirection, a new map condition

New ARC

  • Bully
  • Skulker
  • Hydra

New weapons

  • Bantam
  • Stiletto
  • Amplified Weapons, upgraded versions of existing weapons

New gear

  • Grappling Hook
  • Basic and Advanced Camera
  • Tether Launcher
  • Yank Grenade
  • Mountaineer's Detector
  • Banjo and Harmonica

The Outpost

  • New settlement with a Research Bench
  • Lootable Design items unlock craftable furniture (about 100 designs)
  • Celeste now trades materials for furniture and the new gear

Progression

  • Skill Tree fully revamped
  • Reward Pass replaces Raider Decks; Cred is removed and replaced by Reward Points
  • Frozen Trail Pass: 60 levels, Free and Premium tracks
  • 22 new quests; stash size +2 levels (328 max without Expedition bonuses)

Weapon Stencils

  • 14 stencils: 5 Premium Pass, 1 DLC, 8 found Topside
  • Learn like blueprints, apply to compatible weapons
  • Applying needs Stencil Parts, found Topside
  • A stenciled weapon can still be looted if you go down

Other changes

  • New Scrambling move for steep slopes
  • Reworked Map Selection screen and gamepad defaults
  • Riven Tides loot value up about 15%
  • You can drop loot to the ground straight from containers

Tools

A pre-raid checklist, a project helper that compares what you need with your tracker, and a side-by-side item compare. Saved in this browser only.

Raid checklist

Tick things off before you drop in. Edit the list to match how you play.

Project helper

Enter what a project, craft or upgrade needs and see how close your tracker is. The needs are not pre-filled, so check them against the game. Format: 200 Metal Parts, 50 Wires

Compare items

Pick two items to see what each breaks down into, using the Recycle or Salvage setting from the lookup tab.

Maps

Pick a map to see its difficulty, unlock requirement, conditions and what changed there. Detailed layouts are on the wiki; each card links to that map's page.
