Flashpoint

Released Mar 31, 2026Version 1.22.0

An endgame ARC operation and new weapons.

Key changes

  • Close Scrutiny map condition with an ARC Operation, the Vaporizer ARC, the Canto SMG, the Dolabra legendary energy shotgun and the Surge Coil deployable.
  • Wasp Hunter and Brigade sets, a High-Gain Antenna project, and Shredders on all maps.
  • Locked-room loot value rose with key rarity; Baron Husks drop more loot; Free Augments recycle into 6 Plastic Parts and 6 Rubber Parts.
  • Crafting view lets you acquire missing materials in place by recycling, refining or buying.
  • Rocketeers take less collision damage and Fireflies are less likely to spawn in groups.
