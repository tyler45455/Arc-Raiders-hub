Shrouded Sky

Released Feb 24, 2026Version 1.17.0

Weather, new ARC enemies and a big weapon balance pass.

Key changes

  • Hurricane map condition, two new ARC enemies in the Rust Belt, a free Raider Deck and a Dam Battlegrounds map update.
  • Feats can be rerolled three times a day for free and PvP Feats were removed; Expedition catch-up lets you earn missed Skill Points.
  • Stitcher, Kettle and Venator damage and headshot multipliers cut; Jupiter ADS zoom up to 2.2x; Aphelion reloads and fires faster.
  • Deadline value 5,000 to 6,000 and Trailblazer 1,600 to 2,200; ARC Parts sell value reduced.
  • Strong cheat detections now bring immediate permanent bans.
