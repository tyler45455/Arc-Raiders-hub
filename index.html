ARC Tracker sync

This site can only read what ARC Tracker already has, it cannot make ARC Tracker pull new data from the game. Run a sync in the ARC Tracker Link app first, then press Resync here.

Copies your completed quests, stash, workshop levels, learned blueprints, project stages and raid history from ARC Tracker. ARC Tracker gets them from your game account with its Link app. Skill trees are not in ARC Tracker's API, so set those on the Skills page. This site only reads them from ARC Tracker and never touches the game.

In ARC Tracker, open Settings, then Developer Access, and create a key with every permission ticked (Profile, Quests, Stash, Hideout, Projects, Rounds and Blueprints). Paste it here. A key with fewer permissions still works; the parts it cannot read are skipped. After the first sync, the site syncs by itself each time you open it. The key stays in this browser and is only sent to arctracker.io.

What ARC Tracker sent for your profile and raids

      Back up or move your data
      
Reset everything to default (the red button below) clears quests, tracker, goals, projects, checklists, notes, pins, loadouts and every other saved setting back to zero. It keeps your language, theme and contrast choices. Export a backup first if you might want it back.

      
Your tracker, goals, starred items, projects and checklist are saved only in this browser. Press Download file to save a backup, then use Import from file on another device. You can also press Export to get the same backup as text and paste it here.

Weapons & gear

Sell prices are in Coins; weapons list upgrade levels I to IV. Rarity, weight and sell price for gear come from each item’s own wiki page. Grenade damage is as listed on the wiki; a dash means the grenade does no listed damage.

Tools

Daily and weekly tasks

Compare weapons

Stash space planner

      
An estimate only: the wiki does not list stack sizes, so this counts slots you type in. Your stash size is in the game’s stash screen.

      

        

        

        

      

      

      

      

    


    

      
Keep or recycle?

      
Sorts every item using your active quests, saved projects and tracker goals. Not a replacement for judgement, but it catches the items you would regret recycling.

      

    


    

      
Compare items

      
Pick two items to see what each breaks down into, using the Recycle or Salvage setting from the lookup tab.

      

        
        
      

      
      

    


    

      
Pre-raid planner

      
Pick a map. It uses what you still need to suggest rooms to visit, ARC enemies to hunt and quests that fit.

      
      

    


    

      
Weekly goals

      
Set a target for the next 7 days. Progress counts what you add to the tracker from now on.

      

        

        

      

      

      

    


    

      
Squad board

      
Split up who brings or farms what. Copy the link and send it to your squad, and they can load it into their own board.

      

        

        

      

      

      

      

    


    

      
Crafting planner

      
Pick something to craft and how many. It works out every part down to basic materials, uses components your tracker already has, and lists the workbenches you need.

      

        

        

      

      
      
      

    


    

      
Loot value

      
What is worth the space in your bag. Ranks items by sell price per kilogram or per stash slot (a full stack). Items your active quests, projects, goals or Keep tags need are marked so you do not sell them by mistake.

      

        

        

      

      
      

      
Price changes 

    


    

      
Skill build planner

      
Laid out like the in-game tree. Tap a skill to add a point; a skill stays locked until the one before it has a point, and the large nodes also need 10, 30 or 40 points in that tree. You earn 76 points from levels, plus up to 5 per completed Expedition.

      

        

      

      

      

      

      

      

    


    

      
Raid log

      
Log each raid in a few taps to see your extract rate, average haul and profit on every map. Add what your kit was worth (or pick a saved loadout) and profit is the haul minus the kit; a death counts as losing the kit. Saved in this browser and included in backups.

      

        

        

        

        

        

        

      

      

      

      

    


    

      
Stash worth

      
What the items in your tracker would sell for, and which spare ones to sell first. Spare means more than your active quests, projects, workshop upgrades and goals need (the same needs as the Shopping list). Items you tagged Keep are never listed.

      

        

      

      

    


    

      
Weapon upgrade path

      
Pick a weapon, the tier you have and the tier you want. You get every Gunsmith step, the total materials and what your tracker still needs.

      

        

        

        

      

      
      

      

    


    

      
Quest route planner

      
Groups your active quests into raids, busiest map first, so you finish several per trip. Quests that work on any map fill the spare room. Tick a quest when it is done and the plan updates.

      

        

      

      

    


    

      
Shots to kill

      
Pick an ARC enemy to rank every weapon by how fast it deals that enemy’s health, or pick one weapon to see the shots and magazines it needs.

      

        

        

        

      

      

    


    

      
Shopping list

      

      
Built automatically from your active quests, saved projects, workshop upgrades and tracker goals, compared with what you track. Type how many you have in each box to update your stash counts.

      

        
        
        
        
      

      

      
      
      

    


    

      
Workshop planner

      
Tick how many levels of each station you have finished. Pick how far to plan ahead: those levels go on the shopping list (untick “Include” to leave a station out), and the upgrade path below lists them in order with everything they need and where to find it.

      

    

  


  
  

    
Maps

    
Pick a map to see its difficulty, unlock requirement, conditions and what changed there. Detailed layouts are on the wiki; each card links to that map's page.
