Frozen Trail 2.0

Released Oct 8, 2026Version 2.0Latest update

The biggest update since launch: a new map, new enemies and a rebuilt progression system.

Key changes

  • New map Pendola Pass, with the Frigate and Redirection map conditions. It is the only map testing Dynamic Loot Zones, where zone loot values vary between raids.
  • New enemies Bully, Skulker and Hydra; new weapons Bantam and Stiletto; plus the Grappling Hook, Tether Launcher, Yank Grenade and cameras.
  • Skill Tree revamped with new skills and binary mid-branch choices.
  • Reward Pass replaces Raider Decks, Cred is replaced by Reward Points, and Feats are split into Easy, Medium and Hard.
  • New Outpost unlocked through a project, with a Research Bench, 100 furniture designs and 14 Weapon Stencils.
  • Stash gains two more upgrade tiers (328 slots before Expedition and Nomadic Envoy bonuses); Solo vs. Squads removed from matchmaking.
  • ARC explosive damage reworked: more against armor plates, less against fragile parts. Anvil raised from Green to Blue rarity. Riven Tides loot value up about 15%.
