Headwinds

Released Jan 27, 2026Version 1.13.0

Start of the Escalation roadmap.

Key changes

  • Solo vs. Squads: level 40+ solo Raiders can join the Squads queue for +20% bonus XP.
  • Trophy Display: a five-step project where you submit ARC parts to build a Display Case; full completion gives a Howl emote, a guitar and 300,000 Coins.
  • Bird City, a permanent weekly-rotating Buried City condition with more bird traps, ziplines and flying ARC.
  • Two new Epic Augments, seven new quests, Open Parties, squadmate invites and Discord friend invites.
  • Higher-tier weapon chances in weapon cases; a three-strike ban system (30 days, 60 days, permanent) is being implemented.
