Nomadic Envoys

Released May 19, 2026Version 1.29.0

A new trader and the Rascal grenade launcher.

Key changes

  • New trader Ermal, a Nomadic Envoy, arrives in Speranza at Level 25 with expanded Stash space, Expedition Vault access and weekly rotating offers.
  • New weapon Rascal, a compact grenade launcher for anti-ARC fights.
  • Tactical Mk. 3 (Healing) became an area effect (healing 20 to 45, cooldown 30s to 45s); Combat Mk. 3 (Flanking) accepts Medium Shields.
  • Bettina fire rate raised from 235 to 250; Photoelectric Cloak power cost halved.
  • You can block players after reporting them; Denuvo Anti-Cheat begins a limited rollout.
